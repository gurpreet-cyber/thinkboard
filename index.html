How it works

Move around

Drag empty space to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Double-tap empty space to fit everything on screen.

Add things

Use the bottom bar to add a sticky, text, an image or video, a link or a board. Tap a note twice to edit it. Markdown works.

Select several

Tap the loop button and draw around items. On a computer, hold Shift and drag. Then group, duplicate, move to a nested board or delete them.

Connect and draw

Connect: tap the connect button, then two items. Draw: tap the pencil, and tap it again to stop.

Mistakes

Undo and redo are at the top. After deleting, an Undo button appears for a few seconds.

Offline

Everything works offline. Only YouTube and web page previews need a connection.
